# Photo attributions / 照片署名与许可

All bundled photographs are real photographs from Wikimedia Commons. Original image files are retained without pixel edits. Thumbnails are Wikimedia-generated resolutions. The fern-study paper background is isolated only at display time. Geometry annotations are approximate and are reproducible using scripts/build_examples.py.
